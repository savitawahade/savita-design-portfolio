Some days, the win is finishing a sketch. Other days, it’s opening the sketchbook at all.

Diddit is a personal project I started from a thought I couldn’t quite leave alone. We share our coffee, our outfits and our weekends, but the things that took a little effort often stay unnoticed. I wanted to give them a place too.

I’m designing a habit tracking app where reading a few pages, moving for fifteen minutes or making time for yourself counts as something worth celebrating. You can check in, look back at your progress and cheer on a friend who’s showing up in their own way.

And because a progress bar can only say so much, I created Pip. A small companion with a simple job: root for you. Celebrate when you finish, help you start when you feel stuck and remind you that missing a day doesn’t mean you’ve lost your way.

The warm oranges, soft cards and playful details are my way of bringing that feeling into the screens. I want opening Diddit to feel like a little invitation to try.

I’m also exploring how a completed habit could become an Instagram Story card. Maybe today’s story isn’t somewhere you went. Maybe it’s something you finally did.

This personal project is still taking shape in Figma, giving me room to explore the visual design, interactions and personality at my own pace. The thought behind it is simple: you don’t have to do something big to feel proud of yourself.
